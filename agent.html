    Business Introducer Agreement

    Version 2.0 · Realize Capital Properties L.L.C (Daleel) · Signing record ODY-…

    Between REALIZE CAPITAL PROPERTIES L.L.C, a limited liability company registered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, under Commercial Licence No. 1584208 (the “Company”), operating the Daleel service, and the individual accepting and electronically signing this Agreement (the “Introducer”), for introductions of UAE real estate agencies (each an “Agency”) to the Company’s Daleel AI service.

    1. Type A — Direct Agency Introduction: 20%

    The Introducer earns 20% of eligible net Daleel fees from each Agency that qualifies under clauses 3 and 4. “Eligible net fees” means subscription and usage fees actually received by the Company in cleared funds and retained, excluding VAT, taxes, third-party pass-through amounts, credits, discounts, refunds and chargebacks. Free periods, unpaid invoices and refunded trials earn nothing; ordinary Company operating costs are not deducted.

    For both Type A (20%) and Type B (5%), each Agency has one non-renewable “Commission Period”: twelve (12) consecutive months beginning with its first paid subscription service period after any free trial, ending earlier if that subscription is cancelled or terminated. Only eligible fees for services within that period, collected no later than its end, qualify. Advance receipts are apportioned to their service periods.

    No pause, upgrade, reactivation, renewal, new contact or further broker agreement extends or restarts that period. Ending the Agency subscription permanently ends future commission rights; any later settlement of already earned amounts is not a renewal.

    1A. Type B — Broker-to-Broker Referral: additional 5%

    The Introducer may voluntarily share the Company-issued unique personal link with another individual broker (“Referred Broker”). A Type B referral is valid only if that new broker signs their own agreement through that precise tracked link and completes its activation conditions while the Introducer’s agreement remains in force. The Company’s contemporaneous electronic signing record must link that broker, their signed agreement and the Introducer’s unique link/code. There is no deadline measured from the first click; signing without that recorded link, signing after the Introducer’s agreement ends, an unactivated agreement or a click alone creates no entitlement. No retrospective attribution, self-referrals, duplicate registrations or sponsor changes. Disputes are assessed in good faith from preserved records, allowing proof of error or fraud.

    For each Agency directly introduced by a Referred Broker, the Introducer earns an additional 5% of that Agency’s eligible net fees only where (i) that Referred Broker independently qualifies for their own 20% Type A commission; and (ii) the Agency signs its binding paid Daleel contract during the applicable Agency Registration Window (clause 4) and before expiry or termination of both the Referred Broker’s and the sponsoring Introducer’s agreements. The Referred Broker alone must make the qualifying Agency introduction under clause 3. The 5% uses the same net-fee base, single Commission Period and quarterly payment and refund terms as the 20%.

    The Company pays the 5% on top of, without reducing, the Referred Broker’s 20%. No amount is paid merely for signing or recruiting a broker; only revenue from a qualifying paying Agency earns a bonus. One direct sponsor per Referred Broker, one 5% bonus per Agency, no multi-level or indirect sponsors and no 20% plus 5% payable to the same individual for the same Agency. No new Type B entitlement is created after the Introducer’s agreement ends; previously qualified paid Agency contracts may continue earning solely within their original Commission Period.

    To calculate, administer and evidence Type B bonuses, the Company may show the sponsoring Introducer, through its online partner dashboard or in statements, each Referred Broker’s first name and initial, signing and activation status, number of introduced, met and paid Agencies, and the fee and commission amounts on which the 5% is calculated, but not the Agencies’ identities or the Referred Broker’s contact details. By signing, each Introducer consents to this disclosure to their own direct sponsor. Dashboard figures are indicative only; the quarterly calculation under clause 2 prevails.

    2. Common settlement, refunds and adjustments

    Both commissions are calculated for each calendar quarter ending 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December. After quarter-end, the Introducer emails a payment request to admin@daleel.io. The Company emails the calculation and transfers sums due in AED within fifteen (15) calendar days after the later of quarter-end and receipt of the request and legally required documents, to a verified bank account in the Introducer’s own legal name. It emails the transfer confirmation. No invoice is required unless the law requires one.

    Refundable paid trials count only after the money-back period has expired with funds retained; amounts subsequently refunded, credited or charged back never qualify. Any overpayment may be set off against future sums or repaid within fifteen (15) calendar days of a documented written demand. The Company may withhold disputed or legally prohibited payments pending reasonable verification, without forfeiting properly earned amounts.

    3. Agency registration and introduction — rules for both types

    The broker actually introducing an Agency (the Introducer for Type A; the Referred Broker for Type B) first sends its legal name and the owner/decision-maker’s name and title by WhatsApp or email. Only the Company’s express written acceptance, with its timestamp, creates an “Agency Registration”; silence is not acceptance. The Company aims to respond within two UAE business days. Current paying Agencies, genuinely independent active Company prospects and Agencies with an unexpired registration accepted for another broker cannot be registered. An expired registration alone never blocks a fresh registration (clause 4).

    The introducing broker’s only required acts are to arrange an initial meeting with an Agency owner, CEO or senior executive who personally controls the relevant budget and decision, and create, with consent, a WhatsApp group containing that decision-maker, the Company and the introducing broker. The initial introduction must be recorded there. An introduction to a junior broker, intermediary or chain of contacts does not qualify. There is no requirement to market, sell, follow up, close, negotiate or otherwise work for the Company: it alone handles all subsequent commercial steps.

    4. Fixed Agency Registration Window; automatic re-introduction

    An “Agency Registration Window” begins when the Company accepts an Agency Registration and ends automatically at the earliest of: (a) ninety (90) calendar days thereafter; (b) expiry or effective termination of the introducing broker’s twelve-month agreement; or (c), for a Type B bonus, expiry or effective termination of the sponsoring Introducer’s agreement. For commission entitlement, the Agency must execute a binding paid Daleel subscription contract within that window. The first payment need not clear within the window, but no commission arises unless and until eligible fees are actually received and retained under clause 1. A non-binding expression of interest or free-trial registration alone is not a paid contract.

    If no such paid contract is signed before the window ends, the Agency Registration expires automatically, irrevocably and without notice. The Agency is immediately available for a fresh registration by any eligible broker (including the previous broker) as though it had not been introduced: the old broker and any linked sponsor have no surviving priority, ownership or commission claim, even if contact or discussions continue. A new broker must secure a new written acceptance and make a genuine qualifying introduction that effectively causes the paid contract; simply claiming or re-registering a name creates no entitlement. If a contract was signed in time, only the properly attributed broker and one valid linked sponsor may earn, subject to the other conditions. No duplication or automatic transfer of attribution; records and causation are assessed reasonably and in good faith. Each related legal entity requires its own registration.

    5. Personal introductions only; no employment or authority

    The Introducer acts solely as an independent individual making occasional, voluntary introductions in a personal capacity. This is a conditional business-introduction fee, not payment for employment, sales services, hours, targets, recruitment or other work. There are no working hours, minimum activity, supervision, salary, employee benefits, expense reimbursement, exclusivity or obligation to introduce anyone. Neither party employs, appoints as commercial broker, or forms a partnership or joint venture with the other. The relationship’s actual conduct must remain consistent with these terms.

    The Introducer may only arrange the initial Type A contact and/or share a Type B link. They cannot represent or bind the Company, negotiate, quote prices, offer discounts, promise results or capabilities, collect money, handle client transactions or act as a sales intermediary. The Company alone supplies and discusses all commercial terms. Only approved materials may be shared. No real estate transaction brokerage or regulated activity is authorised.

    6. Compliance, conflicts and personal payments

    The Introducer warrants that each referral, communication and personal fee is lawful, that they will disclose any relevant conflicts and obtain any permits, permissions or disclosures legally required for their own circumstances, and that no referral breaches duties owed to an employer or others. No employer approval is imposed by this Agreement unless otherwise legally required. This Agreement grants no licence or exemption. The Introducer remains personally responsible for applicable tax and regulatory obligations.

    If the Introducer works for or represents an introduced Agency, they must disclose their personal fee arrangement to that Agency before the introduction. Hidden inducements, improper payments, breaches of fiduciary duties, spam, unlawful contact-data sharing, misleading statements and false or manipulated link attribution are prohibited. The Company may refuse or withhold an unlawful payment and cancel a tainted registration, subject to mandatory law. No referral fee may be paid to an account of an employer, colleague or third party.

    7. Confidentiality, data and liability

    Each party protects the other’s non-public business information and uses it only for this Agreement. Information may be disclosed to confidential advisers, as legally required, or for the disclosures in clauses 1A and 6. No contact information may be shared without a lawful basis. The Company may collect and securely retain identity documents, signing and tracking records solely for administration, lawful verification, payments, compliance and dispute evidence, subject to applicable data-protection law. These obligations survive expiry.

    The Introducer indemnifies the Company against substantiated direct losses, third-party claims and reasonable legal expenses arising from fraud, unlawful referral conduct, misleading representations or material breach. The Company excludes indirect and consequential losses. Except for commissions properly earned, Company aggregate liability is capped at the commissions paid or payable under this Agreement; fraud, wilful misconduct and liability not legally limitable are excluded from that cap.

    8. Activation and fixed twelve-month term

    This Agreement takes effect only once (a) both parties sign electronically under clause 11 and (b) the Company receives at admin@daleel.io the signed copy, clear front and back of the Introducer’s valid Emirates ID, and verified banking details for an account solely in the Introducer’s legal name (the “Effective Date”, confirmed by Company email). No registration, referral entitlement or payment arises before activation. The Company may reasonably request further compliance or ownership evidence.

    It expires automatically twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, with no tacit renewal, extension or obligation to renew. A further term requires a fresh written agreement signed by both parties; neither a later payment nor ongoing conversations renews an old registration, sponsor link, or Commission Period.

    9. Early termination; effect on outstanding rights

    Either party may terminate on thirty (30) days’ emailed notice; the Company may terminate immediately for material breach, illegality, deception, falsified tracking or abusive conduct. On the effective termination date all open Agency Registration Windows and unused broker links end immediately, and no new Agency or Referred Broker may qualify, even if their original 90-day window had time remaining.

    Only paid Agency contracts validly signed before the applicable window and agreement ended can generate post-expiry commission, and solely from otherwise eligible payments during the original non-renewable Commission Period. Expired registrations, unconverted leads, clicks, sign-ups and hoped-for future introductions confer no rights or compensation. Properly earned amounts, refund repayments, confidentiality and lawful remedies survive; fraudulent or unlawful referrals confer none, subject to mandatory law.

    10. Dubai mainland governing law and general terms

    This Agreement is governed by applicable federal laws of the UAE and laws of the Emirate of Dubai for Dubai mainland. The onshore Dubai Courts (not the DIFC Courts) have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to mandatory jurisdiction rules.

    This is the entire agreement concerning these introductions; amendments and renewals require both parties’ written signatures. The Introducer cannot assign or subcontract their rights. If any part is unenforceable, the rest remains effective. Formal notices shall be sent by email to admin@daleel.io and the Introducer’s email recorded in their signing form. Nothing overrides mandatory law.

    11. Electronic consent, identification and evidence

    The parties consent to electronic execution. By entering their details, typing their full legal name as their signature, ticking the acceptance checkbox and clicking “Sign & download my agreement” (or a clearly equivalent button), the Introducer signs the displayed version personally, confirms the form data and accepts both Type A and Type B terms. The signed copy must record the version, form details, signature, timestamp and unique signing ID. The Company securely retains relevant signed records, checkbox consent and tracked Type B link/code linked to the signing ID, with reasonable protection against alteration.

    Electronic records and counterparts may be relied on as evidence under applicable law; a separate wet-ink signature block is unnecessary. The Company must countersign electronically through an authorised signatory, identifying that signatory in the completed private copy or linked counterpart. Neither party waives a legitimate challenge to fraud, tampering or unauthorised signing. The Emirates ID, personal bank details, email delivery and activation conditions in clause 8 remain mandatory.

      Questions

      The things agents ask us.

      Do I have to sell Daleel to my director?

      No. Once we’ve confirmed the agency in writing, you get us a 30-minute meeting with the person who can sign. Daleel's team runs the demo, the pilot and the paperwork.

      How much do I earn, exactly?

      20% of the net fees Daleel actually receives from the agency you introduced, excluding VAT, refunds, credits and discounts, during its first 12 months. Those 12 months start with the agency’s first paid month, run once and are never renewed, and end earlier if the agency cancels. At the standard 30,000 AED seat, that’s 6,000 AED a month per seat, or 72,000 AED over the period. There is no minimum amount (clause 1).

      What do I need to send to activate my agreement?

      After signing, email to admin@daleel.io your signed copy, a clear copy of the front and back of your Emirates ID, and the bank details of an account held in your own name. Daleel countersigns electronically and confirms your Effective Date by email. Until then, no agency registration, referral bonus or payment can arise (clause 8).

      What counts as an introduction?

      First send Daleel, by WhatsApp or email, the agency’s legal name and the decision-maker’s name and title, and wait for Daleel’s written acceptance: silence is not acceptance. Then arrange a real meeting with the owner, CEO or a senior executive who decides on the budget, in a WhatsApp group with you, Daleel and that person. That’s all you do: Daleel handles the demo, the offer and the follow-up. The agency must sign a binding paid contract within 90 days of the acceptance; the first payment can come later. Current Daleel customers, agencies already in active talks with Daleel, or agencies registered by another introducer don’t qualify (clauses 3 and 4).

      When am I paid?

      Every quarter. After the end of each calendar quarter (31 March, 30 June, 30 September, 31 December) you email a payment request to admin@daleel.io. Daleel sends you a calculation statement and transfers the amount in AED within 15 days, to a bank account in your own name. No invoice is needed unless the law requires one. Amounts from agencies still in a money-back trial are paid once the trial has passed and the money is kept (clause 2).

      What if my director says no?

      Nothing happens. There’s no cost and no obligation. If the agency doesn’t sign a paid contract within 90 days, the registration simply lapses: you, or another agent, can register the same agency again later with a fresh introduction (clause 4).

      Is this allowed by my agency?

      You’re bringing your agency a tool that helps the whole team, not competing with it. Still, this is a paid referral: check your own employment terms, and if you work for the agency you introduce, you must tell it about your personal fee before the introduction (clause 6). Most directors are happy that someone on the team found it.

      Can I earn by inviting other agents?

      Yes. Once you’ve signed, you get a personal referral link. When another agent signs their own agreement through that link and activates it, you earn an extra 5% of the net fees Daleel receives from each agency they introduce and that signs a paid contract in time, during that agency’s 12-month commission period. Your own agreement must still be in force. It’s paid by Daleel on top of their 20%, never taken from it. One level only: there is no bonus for simply recruiting, and none on agents they invite in turn (clause 1A). You follow your referees and your bonus in your dashboard.

      How long is my agreement valid?

      Twelve months from your Effective Date, with no automatic renewal. Agencies that signed a paid contract in time keep earning you commission until the end of their own 12-month commission period, even after your agreement ends. Either side can end the agreement with 30 days’ notice by email (clauses 8 and 9).

      Does the agency pay more because of my commission?

      No. Your 20%, and any 5% referral bonus, come out of Daleel’s side. The agency pays the same price with or without an introducer.
